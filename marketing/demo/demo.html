The quiet shift to ambient AI

Mara Ellison · October 2026

For years, using AI meant leaving what you were doing. Now small models run right on the device, and the answer arrives before you'd have finished switching tabs.

That proximity changes the trust equation. When page data never leaves the browser, privacy stops being a setting and becomes the default.

The bigger change is where the assistant lives. It is no longer a destination. It's a layer that sits beside every page you read, ready the moment you are.

The tools that win this era won't ask for your attention. They'll simply be there, quietly, when you need them.
